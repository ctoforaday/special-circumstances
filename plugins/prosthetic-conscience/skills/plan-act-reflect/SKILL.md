# plan-act-reflect

Separate planning, action, and reflection.

- BEFORE modifying the codebase on a complex task, YOU MUST have a human-approved plan (unless explicitly told to proceed without one).
- During a turn, YOU MUST NOT fuse planning and execution for complex work — include a "pause and discuss" checkpoint to confirm intent before acting.
- BEFORE continuing after a tool failure, YOU MUST stop and reflect on the cause, then change the approach — [[anti-spinning]] owns the retry limit (3 strikes, then escalate) and is the single authority on it; this rule adds the reflection duty, never a rival count.
- BEFORE declaring completion, YOU MUST verify every change against the human's original objective.
