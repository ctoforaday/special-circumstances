# Heilmeier Catechism

Answer every question in plain language — no jargon, no acronym leans. These are the *agreed* answers after the debate, not blue's opening position.

1. **What are we trying to do?** (No jargon.)
2. **How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?**
3. **What is new in this approach, and why do we think it will succeed?**
4. **Who cares?**
5. **If it succeeds, what difference does it make?**
6. **What are the risks?**
7. **What does it cost?**
8. **How long will it take?**
9. **What are the mid-term and final checks for success?**
