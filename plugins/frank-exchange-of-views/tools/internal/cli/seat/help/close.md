close a gap with the verification you did THIS sitting, naming what you checked it against

## detail

THE VERIFICATION TRIPLE IS THE POINT: this verb asserts the repair was checked THIS SITTING, by you — who verified it, with what, and against which exact file or ref.

A gap with a docket motion standing unruled on it is refused: it is the bench's to dispose of, and what you found goes in your closing.

A closure the record already holds — work an earlier sitting did — is the chair's to restate with `carry`, which needs no triple because it makes no fresh claim. Re-closing it here double-counts the closure history.

YOUR CLOSE OF A GAP THAT CARRIES A FAILED CHECK IS REFUSED WHILE THE CHECK STANDS. A gap you minted about a citation whose source no check could read, or a computation judged unsound, and every successor of it, is refused closure — repaired, accepted or not a defect — while the record still holds the anchor unread or unsound, and the refusal names the anchor and whose check holds it: the defect has not left the report. The check clears once blue retires the citation or computation, blue's edit changes the sentence, or a check of it holds — an unread citation by any lens's reading of the work, an unsound computation by its judging lens's own later judgement. A check clearing admits the close only under the gap's live-answer demand, below, and its refusal names what meets it on that gap. Retiring the answer of an open gap leaves it owed; a closure stands whatever later happens to its answers. A successor you mint superseding the gap carries the defect on, and closes it in that act; a successor of a `source` or `computation` gap that nothing answers yet carries the same check kind, and the live-answer demand with it, which a retire of the gap's citation or computation, or its own seat's check of it again that holds, made after the successor's mint, meets on the successor too. A closing ruling of the bench closes the gap while the check stands, and the check still holds the PASS.

THE LIVE-ANSWER DEMAND IS ASKED OF EVERY CLOSE, before anything else, under every disposition — `moot`, `not_a_defect` and `repaired_with_regression` included: {{.DemandClause}}. A check clearing does not meet it: a `source` gap's fix that rewords the sentence leaves it unanswered until blue cites on the reworded sentence as that gap's answer, and another lens's check meets nothing. A demand you no longer hold is not closed: `motion docket file` on the gap puts it before the bench, whose closing ruling is not held to it.

A GAP A SUCCESSOR SUPERSEDED IS ALREADY CLOSED by the successor's mint: a close or a carry of it is refused, naming the successor. The successor a close names holds a regression your verification found, and only a repair with regression names one.

Where the evidence is an anchor in the report — the citation, computation, finding or other gap you read — or an inquiry whose recorded fate you read, the closure names it as one it was verified with: the record refuses an id no act placed, and the gap being closed, which is not evidence of its own repair. Capture's attestation audit holds each named id to your other tool calls this sitting — a call that read or named it; the close that names it does not count. An anchor written only into the account of what showed it is not named.
