## detail

THE VERIFICATION TRIPLE IS THE POINT: this verb asserts the repair was checked THIS SITTING, by you — who verified it, with what, and against which exact file or ref.

A closure the record already holds — work an earlier sitting did — is the chair's to restate with `carry`, which needs no triple because it makes no fresh claim. Re-closing it here double-counts the closure history.

YOUR CLOSE OF A GAP THAT CARRIES A FAILED CHECK IS REFUSED WHILE THE CHECK STANDS. A gap you minted about a citation whose source no check could read, or a computation judged unsound, and a gap your reading minted on a citation whose source it found against the sentence, are refused closure — repaired, accepted or not a defect — while the record still holds the anchor unread, unsound or found against, and the refusal names the anchor: the defect has not left the report. An unread or unsound check clears once blue retires the citation or computation, blue's edit changes the sentence, or a check of it holds. A found-against check clears once your own later reading of the sentence as it stands backs it, blue edits the sentence, or blue retires the citation — another lens's backing reading clears nothing. A close that hands the defect to a live successor gap is recorded. A closing ruling of the bench closes the gap while the check stands, and the check still holds the PASS.

Where the evidence is an anchor in the report — the citation, computation, finding or other gap you read — or an inquiry whose recorded fate you read, the closure names it as one it was verified with: the record refuses an id no act placed, and the gap being closed, which is not evidence of its own repair. Capture's attestation audit holds each named id to your other tool calls this sitting — a call that read or named it; the close that names it does not count. An anchor written only into the account of what showed it is not named.
