## detail

close a gap WITH its verification: --id R2-3 --as closed|closed_with_regression|... --verified-by L1 --verified-with "git show" --verified-against "7bc501e:path" [--superseded-by R3-1] --reason "<what was verified and why it holds>". THE VERIFICATION TRIPLE IS THE POINT: this verb asserts that the repair was checked THIS ROUND, by you. For a closure the record already holds — work an earlier round did, which you are restating rather than re-attesting — use `carry`, which needs no triple because it makes no fresh claim. Re-closing carried work here double-counts the closure history
