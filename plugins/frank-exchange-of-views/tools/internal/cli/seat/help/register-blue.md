A REPAIR OPENS NO SITTING. When your prompt asks you only to put on the record what your last sitting owed and did not file — its position or its revision — register as the repair of that sitting, and everything you put on the record afterwards counts as that sitting's. The tool names your latest sitting as the one you repair, and refuses when that sitting owes nothing, or when you were dispatched again after it began: that is a new sitting, so register as one.
