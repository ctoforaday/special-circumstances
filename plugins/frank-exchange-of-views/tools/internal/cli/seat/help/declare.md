## menu

a holding that binds how the whole record is READ, when the dispute is over what a term MEANS and no gap moves

## detail

A docket ruling cannot carry a holding: `motion docket rule` needs a motion to answer and a disposition that decides one gap's fate.

Do not bury a holding in a ruling's opinion text, the channel least likely to be read.

A ruling of yours that was wrong, on a docket motion or on a petition, is said here too: the ruling stands and no gap moves, and the declaration states how the record is to be read beside it.

A declaration CONSTRUES HOW THE RECORD IS READ; it does not say where a gap stands. Recording one is the only way a holding reaches the other seats: every seat's work list carries every declaration on the record, and beside each gap id one names it shows that gap's status on the board as the list is read. A gap's status is the board's, never the declaration's — name a gap to say what the holding is about, and leave whether it is open or closed to the board, which moves when the gap does while your text does not.
