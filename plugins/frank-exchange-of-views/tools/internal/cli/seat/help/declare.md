## menu

a holding that binds how the whole record is READ, when the dispute is over what a term MEANS and no gap moves

## detail

A holding binds how the whole record is READ and moves no gap, which is why a docket ruling cannot carry it: `motion docket rule` needs a motion to answer and a disposition that decides one gap's fate.

Reach for it when the dispute is over what a term MEANS. Measured: a bench with exactly this holding put it in a petition ruling's opinion text, the channel least likely to be read.
