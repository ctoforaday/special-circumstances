## detail

A holding binds how the whole record is READ and moves no gap, which is why `opinion` cannot carry it: that verb requires a gap id and a fate.

Reach for it when the dispute is over what a term MEANS. Measured: a bench with exactly this holding put it in a petition ruling's opinion text, the channel least likely to be read.
