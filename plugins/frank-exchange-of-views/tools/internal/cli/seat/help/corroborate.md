## menu

go and find a source blue never cited, when the claim has no anchor but the source is obtainable

## detail

THIS IS THE VERB FOR A CLAIM WITH NO CITATION when the source is obtainable — it answers whether the claim is true in the WORLD, where a finding answers whether the TEXT stands up, and it is what makes "nobody cited this" checkable instead of merely raised.

To adjudicate a citation blue DID author, use `verify` instead: it names the citation by its anchor.
