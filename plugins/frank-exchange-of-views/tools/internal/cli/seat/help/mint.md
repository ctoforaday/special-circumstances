## menu

turn lens findings into a graded gap on the board, when a defect is real and belongs there

## detail

The id is TOOL-assigned. A stable key makes a retry idempotent rather than minting the same gap twice.

Screen first with `near-match`: a candidate overlapping a closed gap is a REOPEN, and a reopen carries its lineage rather than arriving as a fresh gap.

FOUND_BY IS CHECKED AT THE WRITE: a label naming no recorded finding is REFUSED, so a role alone (`L1`) or an invented label fails here rather than resolving to nothing.

ESTOPPEL IS ENFORCED, NOT ADVISED: a mint against text blue applied VERBATIM from your own proposal is REFUSED, and the refusal is logged as friction. Argue it on the ORIGINAL gap, where your prescription sits beside your complaint, or mint declaring that gap as its ancestor. Text blue COUNTER-EDITED is blue's authorship and you audit it normally.
