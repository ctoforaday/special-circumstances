## detail

An event that survives aborts, so something you hit is on the record even if the sitting does not finish.

CLOSE THIS CHANNEL EVERY SITTING, including the ones that went well. A clean sitting is NOMINAL — said in the positive, because an entry that says nothing is still an entry, and silence cannot say it: an absent log reads the same whether the sitting was clean or the channel went unused.

SAY WHAT THE ENTRY ASSERTS. The reader triages this channel by FILTERING on that, instead of reading every entry to find out which it was — so the assertion is the field that makes the channel worth reading at all. An impediment you are merely NOTING has its own word, and is not a request for change: it does not have to be actionable, or advisable.

MOST REFUSALS ARE YOURS and belong in no entry: a wrong verb, a wrong flag, a bad quote — take the correction and move on. A refusal naming a verb you cannot find, or a fact the record holds and no view will show you, IS the finding: report it instead of engineering around it, because a workaround leaves no trace and a missing capability then looks exactly like one nobody wanted.

YOUR AUDIENCE IS THE OPERATOR, not the other seats. This channel is read by whoever can retool you; it is not material for the debate, and nothing here is answered by the other side.
