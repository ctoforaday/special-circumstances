## detail

The bench keeps no memory between runs, so what a human should re-examine exists only if it is recorded. This is not a ruling and it moves no gap: it is the bench's own voice, and it lands in the judicial record rather than the research report. A reader who came for the subject is not the audience for what the bench wants re-examined; the human reviewing the run is, and that is the document they open.
