## detail

An event that survives aborts, so a gap you hit is on the record even if the sitting does not finish.

CLOSE THIS CHANNEL EVERY SITTING, including the ones that went well. The explicit empty form says nothing blocked you, which silence cannot say — an absent friction log reads the same whether the sitting was clean or the channel went unused.

MOST REFUSALS ARE YOURS and are not friction: a wrong verb, a wrong flag, a bad quote — take the correction and move on. A refusal naming a verb you cannot find, or a fact the record holds and no view will show you, IS the finding: report it instead of engineering around it, because a workaround leaves no trace and a missing capability then looks exactly like one nobody wanted.
