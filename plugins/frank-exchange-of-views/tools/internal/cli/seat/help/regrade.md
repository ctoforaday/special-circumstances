## menu

move a grade on a gap that already exists, with the reason it moved

## detail

Same-id grade movement, recorded with the reason it moved: the gap keeps its identity and its history rather than being closed and re-minted.

Likelihood grades the CONSEQUENCE — how likely the harm is to land, never how likely the defect is to BE there.

The regrade and its reason render on the BOARD beside the gap, so a grade argued down over three sittings shows the argument rather than only the final number. They do not reach the research report: an argument about a grade is the debate's, and the report carries what the debate SETTLED.
