## detail

The chair's sitting begins here. The verb computes readiness FROM THE BOARD, records one dispatch per party, stores the plan whole, and prints the same plan for you to relay — the workflow dispatches what the record says, not what you say.

THE RELAY IS ONE JSON OBJECT, AND THE VERB PRINTS IT. In the machine form the verb prints that object and nothing else — no `verb`, `ok`, `result` or `standing` around it (the prose form ends with where you stand), and a refusal is the usual refusal envelope. In prose it is the line under `relay`. Your envelope IS that object — its fields, copied whole as the verb printed them, never wrapped in a key, never composed from the prose lines or from the envelope's shape: a composed plan drops what the prose does not spell (`blockers`, `exchanges`) and adds what the verb omits (`occasions` on a lens or on blue).

It is refused until this sitting is open: once a party has sat for your last dispatch, you are in a new sitting, and the harness's bracket opens it. A resumed dispatch carries no bracket, so there your register opens it, and it is owed before you dispatch. Asked again with nobody sat since, it prints the same plan and records nothing new — so asking for the prose and then for the machine form is one dispatch, not two. Ask again as your sitting's last act when you have filed or ruled a motion since you asked, and relay that plan. `verdict` is the verdict this sitting recorded, absent until it records one; recording it prints the plan carrying it, as this verb prints it, and that is the plan you relay. The workflow reads the verdict and what still stands at the end of your sitting off the plan you relay, and capture compares every plan you relay with the plan the record stored last in your sitting, byte for byte as the verb printed it.

Four things make a party ready. A lens that has never sat, or whose last sitting the report head has moved past — never before a report is ingested, never for a second look at a report that has not changed, and not once three sittings in a row with no fresh material mint have retired it. A lens that owes does not retire, so each head move readies it again: something new on a gap it minted since it last sat — blue's answer, an accepted grade motion, the bench's remand, another lens's finding — a contradiction it read, a proof it owns that nobody has judged, a citation or proof it owns that an edit reopened (a citation is the evidence lens's; a proof is the computation lens's, or the evidence lens's where the cast seats none). The lens that minted an open MATERIAL gap, and blue, while that gap is below its limits — and for ONE more exchange past them when the bench remanded the gap at impasse: `remand_owed` names those gaps, each with the research direction the remand states, and if that exchange leaves the gap at impasse the verb dockets it for the bench again, while a gap remanded at impasse twice readies nobody. The bench, when a gap has reached impasse — the verb dockets it for the bench itself, the moment impasse is computed — or when the open gaps' grade movement is past its threshold: each docket motion's basis says which. And the seat that owes a blocker another seat must clear: the bench for an unruled petition or a docket motion whose gap has closed, the lens that read a contradicting source no finding raises — once per cause, so a seat that has sat for it since and left it is not readied again, and `why` says so.

The bench's party carries `occasions` — what it is convened for: `petition` (it sits before any other party this epoch) and `docket` (its `gap_ids`, after blue). No other party carries one. The bench's dispatch row records them, and its sitting for each is the sitting whose register names that occasion. `blockers` is every blocker on the list the verdict gate refuses on, each with the seat whose act clears it. `key` is the plan's identity: two sittings whose plans decide the same — the same in everything but `epoch`, `final_sitting` and `verdict` — carry one key. `exchanges` has one entry per gap a party is engaged on: `count` is the exchanges had on the gap in all — it never resets, not at a remand either — and `unresolved` is the engaged sittings the record cannot close, which are not counted and are not a count of zero.

Empty is the termination signal, not an error. With `pass_permitted` true, nothing another seat must clear holds a PASS: what still holds it is yours to clear this sitting — the inquiry review, a spot-check naming each of `stale_areas`, and a ruling on each unruled motion whose gavel is yours (grade, inquiry) — and `show work` marks it blocking. Clear it, then issue the verdict. A blocker another seat must clear — a motion the bench rules, a contradicting source no lens has raised, a citation or proof unchecked since its sentence changed, grade movement past its threshold — keeps `pass_permitted` false, and `why` names it. With `ceiling` true, every open material gap is at its limit — remanded at impasse twice — and the run ends CEILING. With `epoch_limit_reached` true as well, this sitting opens the run's last epoch — `max_epochs`, a term setup records — so the parties the board readies are not dispatched and the run ends CEILING for that reason. `epoch` is the epoch the record has reached, this sitting's, and `final_sitting` is true when it is the run's last: no seat is dispatched after this sitting, whoever the board readies, so a grade or docket motion filed in it is refused — no ruling on it could change how the run ends — and a petition is heard by the bench at the exit. Neither: the run ends UNVERIFIED with this plan on the record as the reason.

A gap that is not material — by its class, or graded below medium where its class goes by grade — readies nobody unless its grade movement is docketed. It stays open on the BOARD, and the report's risk matrix lists it while it is open.
