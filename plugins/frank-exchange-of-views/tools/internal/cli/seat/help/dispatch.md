The chair's sitting begins here. The verb computes readiness FROM THE BOARD, records one dispatch per party, and prints the same plan for you to relay — the workflow dispatches what the record says, not what you say.

THE RELAY IS ONE JSON OBJECT, AND THE VERB PRINTS IT. In prose it is the line under `relay`; in the machine form it is `result`. Your envelope's `plan` is that object, copied whole — never one you compose from the prose lines or from the envelope's shape: a composed plan drops what the prose does not spell (`blockers`) and adds what the verb omits (`occasions` on a lens or on blue).

It is refused until you have registered for this sitting: once a party has sat for your last dispatch, you are in a new sitting — the harness's bracket opens it, or your register where a resumed dispatch carries none — and your register is owed in it before you dispatch. Asked again with nobody sat since, it prints the same plan and records nothing new — so asking for the prose and then for the machine form is one dispatch, not two. Ask again as your sitting's last act when you have filed or ruled a motion since you asked, and relay that plan: the workflow reads what still stands at the end of your sitting off the plan you relay, and capture holds the last one to the record there.
