## detail

The run's terminal determination, and a different question from red's PASS or FAIL.

The verdict is derived from the record — a halt, the chair's PASS, or a board at its ceiling — and the word you give is checked against it. The one word you may assert is UNVERIFIED, for a run that stopped before the record reached a terminal state; your reason is the bench's account of the sitting, and on such a run it is the only evidence of why it stopped.
