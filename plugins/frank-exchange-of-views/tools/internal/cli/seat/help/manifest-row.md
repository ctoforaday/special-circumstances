## detail

One receipt per repaired gap: what you checked, and what checking it showed.

It goes on the RECORD, not in the envelope. It is scored from the record at capture and it reaches the reader — the report carries your manifest, and a gap YOU repaired that carries no row is named there as a repair nobody audited, including its author. A gap the BENCH disposed of is not on that list — nobody repaired it, so no receipt is owed.
