## detail

One receipt per repaired gap: what you checked, and what checking it showed.

It goes on the RECORD, not in the envelope. It is scored from the record at capture and it reaches a reader through the BOARD — `docket.md` carries your manifest, and a gap you repaired that carries no row is named there as a repair nobody audited. It is not in the research report: a receipt is the checking apparatus, and the report carries what the checking showed. A gap the BENCH disposed of is not on that list — nobody repaired it, so no receipt is owed.
