the seat's terminal act: the PASS or FAIL, and the checkpoint that follows it

## detail

The seat's terminal act, and the checkpoint that follows it: the record is mirrored to the recovery copy.

A PASS is REFUSED while anything still blocks it — an open gap, an unruled motion, a line of inquiry unvoted this round. `show work` is the list; `sitting.complete` is true exactly when nothing blocking is left.
