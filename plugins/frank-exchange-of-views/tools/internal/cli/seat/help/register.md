## detail

FIRST ACTION at the seat: `register`, and the ONE call that needs your --seat-id: it binds your seat to you on the record, so every later call resolves it and you never type it again. Nothing else runs until this does. Your run is injected; a --run disagreeing with the one you were dispatched into is refused rather than obeyed
