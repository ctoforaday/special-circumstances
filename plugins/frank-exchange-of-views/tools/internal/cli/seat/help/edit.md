## detail

The ONLY path into blue/report.md — a raw write or a shell edit is denied.

The tool matches your span across the invisible anchor layer and REFUSES a replacement that drops, duplicates or invents an anchor: a finding marker or a citation anchor may travel THROUGH your edit, never disappear in one.

An anchor INSIDE the text you are replacing must be copied verbatim into the replacement. One that merely sits at its edge is not inside it — a quote's trailing punctuation is trimmed before the span is located, so an anchor before a sentence's final period falls outside, is preserved for you, and typing it into the replacement is refused as inventing one. You are not asked to work out which: get it wrong either way and the refusal names the anchor and says which of the two it wants.

Quote enough context that the span matches exactly ONE place, or the edit is refused as ambiguous rather than applied to the wrong site.
