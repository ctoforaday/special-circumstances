## menu

the sitting record — what changed this sitting, once, after your edits have landed

## detail

Singleton per sitting; emit it AFTER your report edits land. It renders as the run's CHANGELOG — the report itself shows the current prose and never its own edit history, because a document narrating what it used to say has made its drafting a second subject.

The event IS the record and there is no file: one run carried a 6,847-byte hand-written changelog beside exactly ONE revision event across three eligible seats, and the parity gap was scored against the seat for a verb no prompt had ever named.
