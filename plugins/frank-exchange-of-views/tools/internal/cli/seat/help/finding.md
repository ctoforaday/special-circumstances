## detail

a lens finding (the tool assigns the L{role}-F{N} label): --key <your local F1> --severity <g> --likelihood <g> --impact <g> --reason "..." --quote "<the exact sentence, quoted from the report and nothing else>". A FINDING IS A DEFECT IN THE TEXT — whether what the report SAYS stands up as written. It is not a verdict on the world. For a claim resting on NO citation, that distinction decides the verb: where the source is one you could go and read, `corroborate` puts it on the record and the finding then says what it showed; where there is nothing to fetch — the source cannot be obtained, no source exists to look for, or the defect IS the writing (a figure with no derivation, an internal contradiction, an unfalsifiable universal) — raise it here. `verify` and `reproduce` judge evidence blue already produced. NOTE THE ASYMMETRY RATHER THAN LETTING IT CHOOSE: this verb cannot fail, needing no fetch and risking no `absent`, and the evidence verbs can.
