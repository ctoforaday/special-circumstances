## The refinement: union-copy needs a companion gate

Both run-4 defects are INVENTED AGGREGATES with no source text to copy — union-copy
cannot prevent them. Companion rule (RECOMPUTE-OR-CITE, folded into the bench
constitution via W2c): any assembly-minted number must name its artifact of record
and be recomputed against it at assembly time; cost.md is regenerated BEFORE
assembly or quoted as provisional. Defects concentrate in assembly-computed
aggregates (rates, totals) — exactly the numbers the scorecard telemetry now
computes by script, which removes most of the reason assembly ever computes one.
