# E0.5h — Claim survival (2026-07-18)

18-claim systematic sample (every 8th footnote occurrence) + corpus denominators.

- CONTACT: 100% of sampled footnoted claims got adversarial contact — but ~50%
  gap-level (logic) and ~50% citation-check ONLY (fidelity, never validity);
  n=18 puts the true never-touched bound as high as ~15%, and the frame
  excludes unfootnoted mechanics entirely. Run-5 ledger covered 51/51 footnote
  labels (100%) vs run-4's 64% — method improvement, not a constant.
- SURVIVAL: gap-contacted claims survived UNCHANGED ~11%; in-substance 94%
  (repairs were precision/propagation/spec-totality; no H1-H5 verdict flipped
  in 5 rounds); ~11% ended the run still contested (deferred to build probes).
  Red's hit rate where it aims is near-total: blue rebutted essentially
  nothing across 9 rounds in two runs.
- Leaf refutation rate of external evidence: ~1.7% of 289 ledger rows.

THE STRUCTURAL BLIND SPOT (feeds lens design + constitutions): the five
sections red NEVER gap-anchored are exactly the self-critical, human-gated,
and self-attested material — both disconfirming passes (claims AGAINST the
design attract no adversary), the /graduate human-gated pipeline, the
self-reported live-probe inventory (graded MEDIUM rather than re-run), and
stop-conditions. Red aims hardest at autonomy-critical surfaces and leaves
the design's own self-criticism essentially untested. Implication: a
steelman-verification duty (is the case AGAINST honest and complete?) belongs
in red's lens set — the catechism audit found the same shape at assembly
(the against-case dropped the highest-impact residuals).

Scorecard bindings: attacked-and-held is low-single-digits-to-teens;
survival-in-substance ~94%; citation-only contact certifies fidelity, not
validity — the two must never be summed as one "coverage" number.
