# E0.5c — Lens seat economics (2026-07-18; runs 4-5, costs reconciled to the cent)

ROLE MAP (fixed, both runs): L1-L4 = citation slices; L5 = logic/completeness;
L6 = dark-side/risk.

## Verdict: role-based skew; round-graduated cut supported; flat cut NOT

- L5 + L6 produce SURVIVING findings at ~$0.95-1.16 each vs $2.65-5.60 for the
  citation slices — 3-5x cheaper — and hold that rate FLAT through every round
  while citation yield collapses 70-80% after round 1 (the surface freezes;
  repairs mint fresh logic/composition surface every round). Run-5 r5's L5:7 /
  L4:0 is this role effect at its terminal point, not an anomaly.
- Severity tracks role: every medium+ on run-4's round-1 board was L5/L6-found;
  run-5's #1 open gap is [L3,L5,L6].
- L4 (back-matter slice) is the MARGINAL seat both runs (worst sole-mint cost:
  $10.60 and $14.87) — yet not dross: it sole-found the git write-gadget.
- L5 is the single most load-bearing seat in the engine.

SUPPORTED CHANGE (the ~30% lever, met at 35-38%): keep L5+L6 every round
(untouchable); keep 4 citation slices in ROUND 1 (yield real: 18 credits each
run incl. all external-drift catches); consolidate to ~2 citation seats from
round 2-3 (spot-check + staleness re-fetch). Saves ~$70 (run 4) / ~$119
(run 5) of red-lens spend. NOT supported: a flat 4-lens run from round 1 —
round-1 citation yield is real and slice count is context-bound (round-1
citation seats already run 44-75 API turns).

HONEST LIMITS: the metric cannot price VERIFICATION COVERAGE — the 65-86
pair/round citation ledger IS the PASS bar's evidence grade and is most of
what L1-L4 do; consolidation must preserve the coverage duty, not just the
finding rate. Run-5 r4's double dispatch inflates its citation costs ~2x.
Run-4 found_by survived ONLY in merge-agent transcripts (the in-place board
rewrite destroyed the repo record — the E0.5b retention finding, live again).
