# proposed gap class — `assertion-without-inquiry` [PROPOSED — not in the registry until adopted]

Coined by `red-merge-r2` during `2026-08-23_sleeper-service-plan`. It is NOT staged into any later run:
an unreviewed class validating a future `--class` is the registry losing the only thing it
means. Adopting it means adding the slug to `feov-memory/class-registry.json` by hand.

- **definition**: A graded claim or risk row is added to the report with no backing line of inquiry, citation, or proof -- asserted into the document rather than researched into it
- **neighbour**: `structure-noncompliance`
- **distinguisher**: structure-noncompliance is about the report's required STRUCTURE (e.g. a risk matrix section) being absent or incomplete; assertion-without-inquiry is about one specific claim within the document having no evidentiary or research trail at all, independent of whether the surrounding structure is compliant
- **first used on**: R2-2

The three fields above are the seat's own words, refused at the write if any were missing
(`record.validateClassNew`), so this proposal is well-formed by construction. What a
reviewer decides is whether it DISCRIMINATES — whether the distinguisher actually separates
it from its neighbour on a case where both are arguable.
