# proposed gap class — `graded-warrant-contradicts-record` [PROPOSED — not in the registry until adopted]

Coined by `red-merge-r2` during `2026-09-02_quadratic-formula`. It is NOT staged into any later run:
an unreviewed class validating a future `--class` is the registry losing the only thing it
means. Adopting it means adding the slug to `feov-memory/class-registry.json` by hand.

- **definition**: A grade, confidence or risk statement whose STATED REASON contradicts a ruling the same document makes elsewhere about that very evidence, or weighs only part of the evidence the document itself records.
- **neighbour**: `derivation-status-overclaim`
- **distinguisher**: Is the defect the STRENGTH of a claim relative to its support (overclaim), or a grade whose attached REASON the same document elsewhere forbids or undercuts (this class)? If deleting the because-clause would leave a defensible claim, it is this class.
- **first used on**: R2-3

The three fields above are the seat's own words, refused at the write if any were missing
(`record.validateClassNew`), so this proposal is well-formed by construction. What a
reviewer decides is whether it DISCRIMINATES — whether the distinguisher actually separates
it from its neighbour on a case where both are arguable.
