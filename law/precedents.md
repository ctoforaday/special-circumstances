## apparatus-count-cadence [AFFIRMED 2026-09-07]
facts: On the 2026-09-02 quadratic-formula run, blue's report stated "Executing all fourteen
  stored scripts ... nine reproduce byte-identically ... while five do not". By the time the bench
  ran that loop itself the store held EIGHTEEN scripts, THIRTEEN reproducing — and the five that
  did not were the same five, by name, with the same exit statuses. The denominator had moved
  because blue deposited round-2 proofs while repairing the sentence that counts them. The same
  mechanism appears twice more on that record: the lines-of-inquiry denominator ran 23, then 26,
  then 31 across three rounds, and blue's own repair note observes that correcting 23 to 26 "would
  have shipped the same defect with a fresher number".
question: Is a count over the run's own apparatus, correct when written and overtaken by the same
  round's later work, a fresh instance of the figure-recount defect class?
holding: No. A count the report states over the run's OWN APPARATUS — programs written, proofs
  deposited, lines of inquiry filed, edits recorded — that was CORRECT WHEN WRITTEN and has since
  been overtaken by the same round's later work is a STALENESS ARTIFACT OF THE MEASURING CADENCE,
  and is read rather than docked. It is a defect only if (a) it was wrong when written, or (b) the
  report presents it as current at a site where a reader acts on it and the drift changes what the
  reader would do.
rationale: Under any other reading the class mints one gap per round FOREVER, because the act of
  measuring the apparatus is itself an act that changes the apparatus — the round that repairs the
  count creates the next instance. That is not convergence but a defect generator with a period of
  one round, and it is precisely the case in which a further judged round has negative yield. The
  value in tension is accuracy of the shipped artifact, and it is protected by the two exceptions
  rather than surrendered: a count wrong when written is still a defect, and so is one a reader
  would act on differently.
scope-limits: Assumes the denominator is INTERNAL to the run. A count over an external artifact —
  cached bytes, a source document, a published paper — has a denominator that does not move when
  the run measures it, and staleness there is an ordinary defect. Does not reach a count that was
  wrong when written. Does not reach a site where the drift changes a reader's action. Distinguish
  wherever the count could be GENERATED rather than authored: see cadence-exemption-ends-at-
  generation, which narrows this holding on exactly that ground.
source: 2026-09-02_quadratic-formula, round 2, holding filed beside R2-2

## cadence-exemption-ends-at-generation [AFFIRMED 2026-09-07]
facts: In round 3 of the same run both parties invoked the round-2 apparatus-cadence holding, in
  opposite directions, and both were right about their own instance. The round-2 holding had named
  generation as "the durable fix" and then asserted, as a fact about the world, that nothing in
  either seat's surface could do it — an assertion the round-3 record contradicted.
question: How far does the apparatus-count cadence exemption reach?
holding: The cadence exemption is available only where an apparatus count is hand-authored BECAUSE
  NOTHING CAN GENERATE IT. Where a seat can deposit a program that walks the directories and checks
  the reconciliation, the exemption does not reach it, and staleness there is an ordinary
  figure-recount defect. A seat that CAN generate a count and re-authors it by hand instead has
  chosen the failure mode.
rationale: The earlier holding existed to stop a defect generator with a period of one round. The
  right end state is not a standing exemption but the disappearance of the generator, so the
  exemption must retreat as generation becomes possible — otherwise it ossifies into an amnesty for
  hand-authored numbers and rewards the seat that declines to automate. This also records a
  precedent-versus-leaf conflict resolved the way the statute requires: the round-2 holding's
  factual premise was contradicted at the leaf, and the leaf won.
scope-limits: Narrows apparatus-count-cadence and disturbs nothing else in it. Assumes the
  generating program is within the seat's actual surface, not merely imaginable; a count nobody can
  generate today keeps the exemption until someone can.
source: 2026-09-02_quadratic-formula, round 3, holding filed beside R3-2

## withholding-needs-both-limbs [AFFIRMED 2026-09-07]
facts: R2-5 was withheld from the bench's docket as "minted fresh this round, so not a persisting
  dispute". The record showed both sides had argued it to closing — red in its round-2 closing
  narrative, blue at length under its own heading in its round-2 position. The same withholding
  then misfired twice more in the same run: R3-1, which red made the headline of its round-3 filing
  and blue the headline of its answer, and R3-4, which red raised against a defect its own seat had
  caused and blue answered under its own heading with a control and a filed line of inquiry.
question: When may a freshly minted gap be withheld from the bench's docket as "not a persisting
  dispute"?
holding: Only when BOTH limbs hold: freshly minted AND not argued by both sides. A gap both parties
  argued to closing is a persisting dispute in everything but mint round, and is docketed.
rationale: An argued gap that reaches no ruling returns to red's verdict pool as though nobody had
  considered it — so red weighs, against certification, a gap whose acceptance check passes. In the
  measured cases that would have returned the round's most consequential finding to the pool with a
  PASSING check. Lineage is a proxy for persistence and a poor one; what the rule is actually for is
  keeping the bench off disputes nobody is having, and two seats arguing to closing is the direct
  evidence the proxy was standing in for.
scope-limits: Assumes "argued by both sides" means argued ON THE RECORD to closing — a filing and an
  answer, not a mention. Does not license docketing a gap neither party has engaged. Does not reach
  withholding on any other ground.
source: 2026-09-02_quadratic-formula, round 2 (R2-5), applied again at R3-1 and R3-4
